Computational Fluid Dynamics

My graduate research focused on Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD), combining mathematical modeling, numerical methods, and scientific visualization to study complex physical systems.

Mathematical Modeling

I have a strong foundation in building models grounded in first principles. My doctoral research examined ferrofluid behavior through a reduced-order model coupling fluid dynamics with magnetic fields — work with potential applications in medicine and imaging.
